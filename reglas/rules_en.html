BLINDSPOTS
You can see everyone else's mission.
Everyone can see yours.
Nobody can see their own.
3–4 playersAges 10+20 min

Game Idea

A cooperative game of dice and deduction for 3 or 4 players.

In BlindSpots, everyone can see your mission, except you. Each player places one or more objective cards so that every other player can read them, while the owner sees only the back. Over several rounds you split colourful dice into groups and choose which group to take. Your teammates know what you need; you must trust their hints and read their choices.

You win or lose together. At the end of the game all cards are revealed. If the team has completed its missions (or collected enough stars, depending on the level), everybody wins.

Everyone sees your mission. Everyone helps you.
Only you are in the dark.

The cards carry no text: only dice, colours, numbers and symbols, so they can be played in any language. The small number in the bottom right corner identifies each card and matches the card list on the separate card sheet.

Setup

These steps describe a free game. When you play the campaign, the level you are playing fixes the colours, cards, dice and stars to win (see Campaign).

  1. Choose the colours. With 3 colours, play with blue, orange and green. With 4, add purple; with 5, add yellow.
  2. Fill the bag. Place 15 dice of each colour in play into the bag. Return the other dice to the box.
  3. Agree on the mission. Decide how many objectives each player receives (1 to 3) and which star values may come up. Then decide what the team needs to win: either every objective, or a total number of stars (see End of the Game).
  4. Deal the objective cards. Shuffle the objective cards that fit your game and deal the agreed number to each player, face down. Leave out cards that show a colour not in play.
  5. Place your cards without looking. Without looking at the front, slide your cards into your card stand so that the front faces the other players. From now on you may look at everyone's cards except your own.
  6. Choose the first dealer. Pick a player at random. Play passes clockwise; each player deals once.
  7. Variants. If you play with the grey die, black dice or action cards, prepare them now (see Variants).
ImportantNever look at your own cards until the end of the game. If you see one by accident, swap it for a new card from the deck.

Playing the Game

A game lasts as many rounds as there are players. In each round, one player is the dealer. The first dealer starts; after each round, the role passes to the left.

1. Draw and roll

The dealer draws dice from the bag at random, without looking, and rolls them:

PlayersDice per round
35
47

Rule of thumb: (players × 2) − 1. Dice that leave the bag never return to it, unless an action says so.

2. Form groups

The dealer splits the rolled dice into as many groups as there are players. Groups do not have to be the same size, but each group must contain at least one die. The dealer may not change the value shown on any die.

3. Choose groups

Starting with the player to the dealer's left and going clockwise, each player chooses one of the remaining groups and places it in their personal area. The dealer takes the last group.

The dealer knows what the others need, but not what they need themselves. Building the groups is the heart of the game: give each teammate a group that helps them, and hope that what is left suits you.

4. Next round

The dice in your area stay there until the end of the game and all count towards your mission. The player to the left of the dealer becomes the new dealer and a new round begins.

Communication

Talking is part of the game, within limits. You may discuss the groups on the table and give advice, but you may never reveal what a card says.

AllowedNot allowed
“I think group B suits you better.”“Your card says: no blue dice.”
“That die could be dangerous for someone.”Reading out, describing or pointing at a symbol on someone's card
“I'd rather not take the green dice.”Telling a player how many stars their cards are worth

When in doubt, ask the table. The spirit of the game: hints, not answers.

Objective Cards

Each objective card states one condition about all the dice in your area at the end of the game. Its stars (1 to 5) show its difficulty and how many stars it is worth when completed. If you hold several cards, together they form your mission.

The bonus star

Cards with an asterisk line at the bottom, such as * =2 → +1★, reward precision. The objective is met with the amount shown or more (or, for “at most” cards, that amount or fewer). If you hit the amount exactly, the card earns 1 extra star.

Comparisons with other players

Cards with a crown compare you with every other player: you must be strictly first. A tie does not count.

Values, colours and special dice

  • Value means the number of pips on the die, from 1 to 6. Even values are 2, 4 and 6; odd values are 1, 3 and 5.
  • Straights are consecutive values, such as 2-3-4-5. A colour straight must be made of dice of one colour.
  • The grey and black dice count as dice, for sums and for values, but never as a colour.

End of the Game

The game ends after the last dealer's round. If you play with black dice, resolve the bombs first (see Variants). Then everyone turns their card stand around and checks their cards against the dice in their area.

The team wins in one of two ways, agreed during setup:

  • Every objective. Every card of every player must be completed.
  • Team stars. Each completed card is worth its stars, plus 1 bonus star for an exact hit. Add up the stars of the whole team. If the total reaches the target, you win.
ExampleAna completes her 3-star card and hits its bonus. Bruno completes a 2-star card and fails a 1-star card. Carla completes a 4-star card. The team scores 4 + 2 + 4 = 10 stars.

In addition, the team loses whatever its score if:

  • the player holding the grey die has not met the grey card, or
  • a player received a second black die (see Variants).

Lost? Shuffle, deal new cards and try again. Every game is different.

Variants

The three variants below are introduced step by step in the campaign. In a free game, you may add the grey die.

Special actions

Setup. With 3 players, shuffle the 4 action cards and place 3 of them face up; return the fourth to the box. With 4 players, use all 4.

Using an action. At any time during the game, any player may use one available action on any visible die: in the dealer's roll, in a group, or in any player's area. Then take the card: it is no longer available to anyone. Each player may use only one action per game.

CardEffect
A1 RerollRoll the die again.
A2 +1 / −1Turn the die to one pip more or one pip less (never below 1 or above 6).
A3 FlipTurn the die to its opposite face: 1 ↔ 6, 2 ↔ 5, 3 ↔ 4.
A4 RemovePut the die back into the bag.
TipAnnounce each action clearly: who you are, which die, whose die it was and its value before and after (“I flip Bruno's orange die: from 5 to 2”). Agree on it as a team: a used action is gone for everyone.

The grey die

Setup. Shuffle the 7 grey cards and place them face down.

First round. The first dealer also rolls the grey die and reveals the top grey card, face up for everyone, including you. The grey die joins the groups like any other die.

End of the game. Whoever holds the grey die must meet the grey card. If not, the team loses, even if it has enough stars. The grey die counts as a die, for sums and for values, but not as a colour.

The black die: the Bomb

Setup. Shuffle the 9 black cards and place them face down. The black cards form a single deck from game to game: a card only comes back once all the others have been drawn. When the deck runs out, shuffle all 9 again.

Each round. Before drawing any dice, the dealer reveals the top black card, so everyone knows its effect before anyone takes the black die. Then they draw coloured dice as the level states (6 with 3 players, 8 with 4), roll them together with one black die and place the card next to that black die. The card travels with the die.

One black die each. Each player may end the game with only one black die. If you already have one, you may not choose a group that contains another.

Careful with the dealerIf the last group contains a black die and the dealer already has one, the team loses the level at once.

The bombs go off. After the last round and before scoring, each player holding a black die, in turn order, applies its card to their own dice and removes them. You choose which dice, among those the card allows. If no die fits the card, nothing happens. The black die itself is never removed by its card. Removed dice go back to the box.

Campaign

The campaign leads 3 or 4 players through 25 levels in five worlds of five levels each. Each level fixes the colours, the objective cards each player receives, the dice in the bag and the stars the team needs. Beat a level to unlock the next one and tick it in the campaign log. If you lose, replay the level with new cards. You may replay any level you have already beaten.

Dealing campaign cards. Sort the objective cards by stars. Each player receives one card of each value shown in the Cards column: “3 + 1” means one 3-star card and one 1-star card. Stars to win: use the value for your player count; add up the stars of all completed cards, plus bonus stars.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a group be empty? No. Every group contains at least one die.

May the dealer see the values before forming groups? Yes. The dice are rolled first and then split as they lie.

Two of my cards seem to contradict each other. Cards dealt to the same player must be compatible. If you deal by hand, reshuffle a card that clearly contradicts another.

Two players have the same crown card. Only one player can be strictly first at the same thing. Replace the second copy.

Does the grey or black die count for “dice of exactly 3 colours”? No. They count as dice and for values, never as a colour.

Can an action target the black die? Yes. If the black die is removed, its card is discarded with it.

The black card says “your lowest die” and I have two 1s. Choose either one.
